# The Loop We Wrote: Time Travel, Gods, and the Four Yugas

*Theorems and Threads, by Shiv Umahiya*

> A speculative essay connecting time-travel physics with the four Yugas. The physics discussed is real; the proposed connection to scripture is a thought experiment, not an established theory.

## The Question That Should Keep You Awake

What if they are instructions? And they are so well disguised that we have been worshipping them for thousands of years?

Here is the theory we shall explore:

Humans discover time travel and gain so much power that they become gods to everyone beneath them. They travel back in time. However, the moment they interact with the past they alter it and become stuck in a loop! This loop is what is referred to in the hindu tradition as the four Yugas. ## First, What Does 'Time Travel' Even Mean?

To make things easy to understand from a physics perspective, there are two types of time:

- **Personal time** ($P_t$, or proper time $\tau$): The time experienced by the traveller, measured by their clock, body, memory, and heartbeat.
- **External time** ($X_t$): The time experienced by the outside world.
In normal circumstances, $P_t = X_t$. You age 1 year, the outside world ages 1 year.
When the two times do not match, time travel occurs: $P_t \ne X_t$.
If $X_t > P_t$, then the time traveller arrives in the future of the outside world.
If the traveller's world line is bent back to their own past, they have travelled backwards in time.

There we have it! Forward time travel is actually a known part of physics. The question is how much can we push it.

## The Mathematics of Slowing Time

Einstein started with the assumption that the speed of light ($c$) is constant in every inertial frame. From this assumption, a clock moving at velocity $v$ has its time dilated by a factor of $\gamma$:

$$\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}.$$
If the traveller's clock reads $\Delta\tau$, then the outside world has aged:

$$\Delta t = \gamma\Delta\tau.$$
For a general world line, the proper time experienced is given by:

$$\tau = \int \sqrt{1-\frac{v(t)^2}{c^2}}\,dt.$$
Let us look at some examples:

| Velocity | Lorentz factor ($\gamma$) | Time elapsed outside for 1 year experienced by traveller |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| $0.6c$ | 1.25 | 1.25 years |
| $0.99c$ | approximately 7.1 | 7 years |
| $0.9999c$ | approximately 70.7 | 70 years |
| $0.999999c$ | approximately 707 | 707 years |

Using the acceleration, cruising and turning around method an astronaut can step out of their spaceship to find the outside world has aged many centuries while they have only aged a few years. This is known as the twin paradox. It has been confirmed using atomic clocks on aircraft and with muons, which live far longer when moving at high speeds.

Time can also be slowed by gravity. Outside a non-rotating mass $M$, the Schwarzschild solution gives:

$$d\tau = \sqrt{1-\frac{2GM}{rc^2}}\,dt.$$
If r approaches the event horizon at $r_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2}$, then the square root tends to zero. Therefore to an outside observer the time experienced by the clock hovering near the black hole nearly stops. If someone spends an hour near the event horizon they could return to find the outside world has aged years.
This is known as the deep gravitational field effect. GPS satellites already account for this effect. Without accounting for it, your map would drift by kilometres every day!

Nothing to worry about so far. Time is very flexible.

## The Puranas Described the Same Mismatch

Here is where the theory really starts to kick in. Three famous stories from the Puranas have the exact Pt ≠ Xt situation.
King Kakudmi and his daughter Revati visit Brahma in Brahmaloka to ask who she should marry. They wait while the Gandharvas finish their song. When they return Brahma informs them that 27 Chaturyugas have passed on earth. The dynasties they knew no longer exist and the world they knew is now dust.

Let us think about this as an engineering problem. $27$ Chaturyugas $=27\times4.32$ million $\approx1.17\times10^8$ years. If the song took 5 minutes of personal time then the time dilation factor would need to be,
$$\gamma \approx \frac{1.17\times 10^8\text{ years}}{5\text{ min}} \approx 1.2\times 10^{13}.$$
The protons in the Large Hadron Collider only have $\gamma\approx7000$. This is nearly two billion times greater than what is needed!

Brahma hides Krishna's cowherds and cattle. Whilst the earth ages a full year the cowherds only age half a second.
$$\gamma \approx \frac{3.16\times 10^7\text{ s}}{0.5\text{ s}} \approx 6.3\times 10^7.$$
Kandu spends time with the nymph Pramlocha getting lost in thought. When he steps out he believes he has aged one day. However, she tells him he has aged 1600 years, 6 months and 3 days.
$$\gamma \approx \frac{1600.5\times365.25\text{ days}}{1\text{ day}} \approx 5.8\times 10^5.$$
All three have different ages but the pattern is consistent. Someone enters a different frame and when they return they find the world has aged. All three situations involve entering a different loka. A loka is a realm. These realms are different locations in the cosmos and therefore have their own clock.

Could this be a coincidence? Or is it a clue?

## A Ladder of Clocks

The Puranas do not just give stories; they give a whole hierarchy of time.
- 1 human year = 1 day/night of the Devas.
- 360 human years = 1 divine year.
- 12,000 divine years = 1 Chaturyuga = 4,320,000 human years.
- 71 Chaturyugas = 1 Manvantara.
- 1,000 Chaturyugas = 1 Kalpa = 1 day of Brahma = approximately 4.32 billion human years. (Some texts describe 14 Manvantaras, giving 4.294 billion.)
This is followed by a night of the same length. During this time the worlds are dissolved by Pralaya.
Brahma lives for 100 years (Parardha) which is ~311 Trillion Human Years.
The Linga Purana goes even further. One day of Vishnu equals the full life of Brahma; one day of Rudra equals the full life of Vishnu.
This creates a geometric hierarchy of reference frames, each with a vastly different rate. A related effect appears in general relativity, where clocks at different gravitational potentials run at different rates.

The four Yugas are:

The ratio between the times is 4:3:2:1. Each age is shorter and darker than the previous. We are told that we live in the last age, the Kali Yuga. The age of decay.

## The Spacetimes That Allow the Impossible

We have solved the forward time travel issue. The theory requires us to do something more difficult. Fortunately general relativity does not prohibit loops.

### Malament-Hogarth spacetimes: surveying infinity

A spacetime $M$ is classified as Malament–Hogarth (M–H) if there exists a future-directed timelike half-curve $\gamma$ and a point $p$ such that:

$$\\int_{\\gamma} d\\tau=\\infty, \\qquad \\gamma\\subset I^-(p).$$
Here, $I^-(p)$ is the causal past of $p$. This means there is a path which takes infinite proper time to traverse, however the entire path is contained in the past of p. An observer Φ can take a world line with finite proper time and reach p. They can then observe the infinite history of the past.
This is the mathematical version of someone with a finite amount of life exploring an infinite amount of external time.
Read the story of Sage Markandeya again. During the Pralaya he is swallowed by an infant on a banyan leaf. He explores all fourteen worlds inside the infant's body and sees the entire history of the universe. His world line was finite but he explored an infinite amount of external history.
Physicists refer to this as an M-H region. The Markandeya Purana refers to this as the belly of Time.
Importantly M–H spacetimes are considered nomologically accessible within classical GR. There is no no-go theorem and there are no logical paradoxes.

### Gödel's rotating universe: the loop

In 1949, Kurt Gödel (Einstein's friend at Princeton) discovered an exact solution to Einstein's field equations:

$$G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}.$$
This describes a rotating universe made up of dust. In the correct coordinates, the metric is:

$$ds^2=a^2\left[dt^2-dx^2+\frac12e^{2x}dz^2-dy^2+2e^x\,dt\,dz\right].$$
With the correct ratio between matter density and negative cosmological constant ($4\pi G\rho=-\Lambda=\frac{1}{2a^2}$), the geometry has some frightening properties. Beyond a critical radius:

$$r_c=2a\ln(1+\sqrt{2}).$$
The light cones tilt so much that a path that always moves forward in time will curve around and return to where it started! This is a closed timelike curve (CTC).

A CTC is a path where you always move forward in time but return to before you started.
Every point in Gödel's universe acts as an M-H region. Two inertial observers will have orthogonal time axes. Time is rotated!
The Vedic description tells us that Kala (time) has a wheel with seven spokes and a thousand eyes (Atharva Veda XIX.53-54). The Yugas rotate around. The Sanskrit word for this is chakra.
Mathematics agrees with the scriptures.

### Wormholes and the machines of the past

Other proposed ways to travel to the past include:

- **Morris–Thorne wormholes:** A tunnel between two regions of spacetime. The metric is shown below. Moving one end at high speed could, in principle, make the two ends become time-shifted due to time dilation. Entering one end and exiting the other has been proposed as a way to reach the past.
- **Tipler cylinders:** An infinitely long cylinder rotating at high speed, causing spacetime to spiral.
- **Mallett's rotating ring of light:** A proposal that circulating intense lasers could twist spacetime and create a loop. This idea is not widely accepted.
$$ds^2=-e^{2\Phi(r)}dt^2+\frac{dr^2}{1-b(r)/r}+r^2d\Omega^2.$$
Three different methods, all returning a world line.

## So Here Is the Theory

1. **Premise 1:** General relativity allows extreme time dilation and contains mathematical solutions with closed timelike curves.
2. **Premise 2:** The Puranas contain stories with a $P_t \ne X_t$ pattern, a hierarchy of time frames, and cosmological imagery of time as a wheel.
3. **Premise 3:** If a civilisation mastered such spacetimes, it might appear godlike to beings beneath it, experiencing a different rate of time.
4. **Conclusion:** Perhaps future humans travelled into the past and became trapped in a loop. Knowing they would be trapped, they left the science in the form most likely to survive for 10,000 years: a myth.
Myths are an incredible way to store information. They are self correcting, easy to remember, and will survive fires, floods and empire collapses. A physics textbook certainly won't!
Suddenly the Yugas no longer seem like a calendar. They are the period of the loop.
Interestingly one kalpa (4.32 billion years) is similar to the age of the earth (~4.54 billion years). I would say this was a coincidence, but it is hard not to notice.

Assume the knowledge came from future humans allowing them to travel to the future. The information has no source. It just exists and will forever. Physicists refer to this as the bootstrap paradox. The oldest depiction is the Worm Ouroboros. The snake eating its own tail. This describes a being which is its own cause. If the time traveller becomes their own parent then they have no beginning or end to their life.
Where did the idea for this blog come from?
Every serious consideration of CTCs results in the same problem. The Novikov self consistency principle states that if you go back in time you can only do what has already happened. There is no changing the past, only fulfilling it.
However if this rule is broken causality becomes probabilistic. Something trivial in the past could cause massive changes in the present. This is known as the Bradbury effect from his short story. Stepping on an insect could cause an election to be rewritten.
Our loop situation suggests the rule has already been broken. The time travelers messed with it. Therefore the world we are in is not the original. It is a fixed version. Fixed versions have seams.
The second law of thermodynamics defines the arrow of time:

$$\frac{dS}{dt}\geq 0.$$
Everything in nature will follow this from your coffee cooling to stars burning. A CTC forces the time traveller to go against the arrow.
Theorists taking this to the extreme have realised that a being stuck in a closed loop trying to avoid entropy will find themselves in a solipsistic state. ## The Cold Water: Why This Cannot Be Done, and Why the Theory Might Be Wrong

I promised to be honest at the end. This theory is exciting so I shall try my best to tear it apart.

### The energy bill is astronomical

The energy required for the Kakudmi requirement $\\gamma\\approx1.2\\times10^{13}$ is:
$$E=\gamma mc^2\approx(1.2\times10^{13})(70)(9\times10^{16})\approx7.7\times10^{31}\text{ J}.$$
The Sun releases approximately $1.2\\times10^{34}$ J of energy in a year. This means accelerating one person to the speeds implied by the Kakudmi story would require about half a percent of the total energy the Sun produces in a year. This does not take into account the energy required to transport the fuel, the shielding from the cosmic microwave background (which becomes deadly at these speeds) or slowing down again. This is not difficult, it is impossible to describe.

### Closed timelike curves need exotic matter

Morris-Thorne wormholes will only stay open if the throat flares. This requires matter with negative energy density violating the null energy condition. The only hint we have of this type of matter is the quantum Casimir effect. This only creates small amounts of negative energy. Not enough or concentrated enough. Tipler's cylinder must be infinitely long. Gödel's universe requires rotation we cannot measure in our universe. The observed universe is expanding whereas Gödel's universe is static.

### Nature may have a chronology police

Stephen Hawking proposed the chronology protection conjecture. The laws of physics conspire to stop closed timelike curves ever forming. This is because if you tried to create a time machine you would cause the quantum vacuum fluctuations to go out of control. The energy would go to infinity as you created it. This is a conjecture not a theorem but is the mainstream expectation.

### Entropy still points one way

General relativity treats time as just a coordinate. Thermodynamics does not. Every system we know obeys the second law. Any physical method of going against the arrow would need to explain how a warm, disordered macroscopic object (you) can reverse it's increase in entropy for free. No known process can.

### Paradoxes are real constraints

The grandfather and bootstrap paradox are not just fun. The universe will either enforce the Novikov principle (making free will an illusion) or it will create parallel worlds (Deutsch's many worlds model). Neither have been shown.

### The mythology might simply be mythology

This is the most obvious reason. The Puranas were written and edited over many centuries. The large numbers may just be exaggerations to show how amazing time is. Or they could be teaching a philosophy about the relativity of our experiences to our conscious. Many cultures have independently discovered the concept of cyclical time. The Maya Long Count to Greek eternal return, and none of them needed a time machine to do it. The parallels I drew are real *as parallels*, but a pattern that fits a story after the fact is not evidence that the story was written to describe it. The approach called *interpretatio technologica*, which translates ancient scripture into modern technology, is famously flexible. It can make almost any text say almost anything. A theory that cannot be falsified is not yet a theory.

### So where does this leave us?

Here is the honest ledger.

- **Established:** time dilation (special and general relativity) is real, measured, and usable. Forward time travel in the strictest sense is already happening, one nanosecond at a time.
- **Mathematically permitted, physically doubtful:** M-H spacetimes, Gödel universes, wormholes, Tipler cylinders. They solve Einstein's equations, but need conditions we have never seen.
- **Speculative:** that anyone has ever used them.
- **Unfalsifiable as stated:** that scripture is a deliberate manual from future humans.

I do not believe the theory is true. I believe it is **worth thinking about**, because the attempt forces you to learn real physics, notice real patterns in a civilisation's oldest writing, and ask what a message from a distant future would even look like.

And if the loop *is* real, then at least one thing is certain: whoever is writing this sentence was always going to write it.
